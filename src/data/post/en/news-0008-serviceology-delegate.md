## Professor Ryosuke Saga elected as an 8th-term representative of the Society for Serviceology

**Professor Ryosuke Saga** of our laboratory has been elected as an **8th-term representative** of the **Society for Serviceology**.

---

### Overview

On May 9, 2026, the election committee for the Society's 8th-term representatives announced the election results. Professor Saga was appointed as one of 20 representatives elected from across Japan.

- **Term**: June 2026 (after the 7th-term annual general meeting) to June 2028 (end of the 8th-term annual general meeting)

---

Alongside our research and teaching, we will continue to contribute to the advancement of service science through our work with academic societies.
