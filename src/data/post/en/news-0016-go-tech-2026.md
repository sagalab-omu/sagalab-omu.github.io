## Research project involving our laboratory selected for the FY2026 Go-Tech Program

A research project in which **Professor Ryosuke Saga** of our laboratory participates has been selected for the **FY2026 Go-Tech Program**, run by the Kinki Bureau of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI).

---

### Research Project

**Multi-vessel operation of interactive "Robosen" autonomous vessels and development of water environment simulation technology based on underwater and seabed data collected by Robosen**

This project will improve "Robosen," an autonomous navigation vessel, so that it can automatically collect data on coastal water quality and 3D seabed topography over wide areas and at high frequency. The data are expected to be used to predict the risk of mass mortality in marine aquaculture such as oyster farming, and the impact of turbid water runoff on coastal ecosystems.

---

### Our Role

Professor Saga is responsible for developing an interactive information management system (HMI) that applies **deep learning AI and generative AI (RAG)**.

---

### Project Structure

- **Project management organization**: Osaka Science & Technology Center
- **Lead research institution**: Nippon Kaiko Co., Ltd.
- **Partner research institutions**: Tokyo Kyuei Co., Ltd.; Osaka Metropolitan University
  - Associate Professor Yasunori Nihei (Graduate School of Engineering)
  - Professor Ryosuke Saga (Graduate School of Informatics)
  - Visiting Associate Professor Satoshi Nakada (Research Center for Advanced Aquaculture)

---

We will continue to put our research into practice through collaboration among industry, academia, and government.
