## We presented our research at CEC 2026 (WCCI 2026)

**Chihiro Noda** from our laboratory attended the **IEEE Congress on Evolutionary Computation (CEC 2026)**, part of the **2026 IEEE World Congress on Computational Intelligence (WCCI 2026)** held in Maastricht, the Netherlands, from June 21 to 26, 2026, and presented our research.

The presentation reported on the accepted paper below, a benchmark-style study at the intersection of evolutionary computation and information visualization.

---

### Presentation

- **Evolutionary Edge Bundling as a Large-Scale Multi-Objective Optimization Problem: A Comparative Study**  
  Chihiro Noda, Ryosuke Saga  
  DOI: [10.1109/CEC69861.2026.11727394](https://doi.org/10.1109/CEC69861.2026.11727394)

---

### Looking Back

After the talk, many researchers told us they found the approach interesting. We also had in-depth and productive discussions on the need to bring in algorithms that are strong on large-scale global optimization (LSGO) problems, and on how dataset size affects the results.

Attending CEC, a top conference in computational intelligence, was a valuable learning experience, and we came away with many new ideas to build into our future research.

---

We will continue to share our research through presentations at international conferences.
