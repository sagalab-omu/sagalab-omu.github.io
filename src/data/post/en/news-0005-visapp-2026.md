### Presentation

- **[Edge Bundling with Divergence and Convergence](https://www.insticc.org/node/TechnicalProgram/VISAPP/2026/presentationDetails/144915)**  
  Naoki Hashimoto, Ryosuke Saga  
  A new method that introduces a convergence process into genetic-algorithm-based edge bundling, achieving both high bundling quality and diversity

---

### Overview

In graph visualization, visual clutter becomes a problem as the number of edges grows. Edge bundling reduces clutter by grouping related edges together. Approaches based on genetic algorithms (GA) can produce a wide variety of bundling styles, but they tend to yield less dense bundles than physics-based methods.

This work proposes a new method that adds a "convergence" process to GA-based edge bundling. It introduces two convergence strategies, **Nearest Neighbor Merging** and **FDEB-like Merging**, both controlled by a single frequency parameter.

Experiments showed that the proposed method substantially outperforms the baseline on bundling quality metrics such as Ink-Ratio, Spatial-Entropy, and Edge-Crossings. They also confirmed that the frequency parameter offers flexible control over bundling strength.
