## Our paper has been accepted at SCIS&ISIS 2026

A paper by **Chihiro Noda** and colleagues from our laboratory has been accepted at the **Joint 14th International Conference on Soft Computing and Intelligent Systems and 27th International Symposium on Advanced Intelligent Systems (SCIS&ISIS 2026)**, organized by the Japan Society for Fuzzy Theory and Intelligent Informatics (SOFT).

SCIS&ISIS 2026 will be held in Hachioji, Tokyo, from November 2 to 5, 2026. The presentation will take place at the conference.

For details on the conference, see:

### Accepted Paper

- **Evolutionary Edge Bundling: Comparing Large-Scale and Many-Objective Evolutionary Algorithms**  
  Chihiro Noda, Ryosuke Saga  
  A comparison of large-scale and many-objective optimization algorithms for evolutionary edge bundling

---

### Overview

Evolutionary edge bundling optimizes the displacement of control points placed on each edge of a network under several aesthetic criteria. Because the number of decision variables grows with the number of edges, the task is inherently a **large-scale multi-objective optimization problem**.

This study extends our earlier comparative study presented at CEC 2026. After normalizing four objective functions, we compared three methods designed for large-scale optimization (**CCGDE3, LSMOF, and LMOCSO**) with two representative many-objective methods (**MOEA/D and NSGA-III**) on four networks with up to 56,895 decision variables.

The experiments showed that the most suitable optimizer depends on the problem size, and that decomposing the decision variables does not work well for this problem.
