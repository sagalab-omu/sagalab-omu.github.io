## Our paper has been accepted at CEC 2026

A paper by **Chihiro Noda** and colleagues from our laboratory has been accepted at the **IEEE Congress on Evolutionary Computation (CEC 2026)**, a leading international conference on evolutionary computation.

CEC 2026 will be held as part of the **2026 IEEE World Congress on Computational Intelligence (WCCI 2026)** in Maastricht, the Netherlands, from June 21 to 26, 2026. The presentation will take place at the conference.

### Accepted Paper

- **Evolutionary Edge Bundling as a Large-Scale Multi-Objective Optimization Problem: A Comparative Study**  
  Chihiro Noda, Ryosuke Saga  
  A comparative study of edge bundling using multi-objective evolutionary algorithms

---

### Overview

Edge bundling is an important technique for reducing visual clutter in network visualization. This study formulates evolutionary edge bundling, which optimizes edge geometry under multiple objective functions, as a large-scale multi-objective optimization problem. It then compares five representative multi-objective evolutionary algorithms (**MOEA/D, SMPSO, NSGA-II, NSGA-III, and SPEA2**) within a single unified pipeline.

By unifying the control-point representation, objective functions, datasets, and evaluation protocol, the study attributes differences between algorithms to their optimization behavior. Experiments on one synthetic network and two real-world airline networks revealed that each algorithm strikes a different trade-off among convergence speed, Pareto-set coverage, and visual diversity. The study offers practical guidance on choosing algorithms for LSGO-type edge bundling tasks.
