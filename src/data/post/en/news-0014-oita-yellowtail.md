## Commissioned research with Oita Prefecture on AI-based yellowtail behavior analysis begins

Our laboratory has started commissioned research for **Oita Prefecture** on **analyzing the behavior of yellowtail using AI**.

---

### Overview

In this project, we will develop analysis methods that use artificial intelligence to study the behavior of yellowtail. We aim to apply the machine learning and data analysis expertise our laboratory has built up to the fisheries sector and deliver results that help solve real problems in the field.

---

We will continue to work with local governments and companies to put our research into practice.
