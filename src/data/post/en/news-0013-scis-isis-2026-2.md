## Our research wins the Best Application Award at SCIS&ISIS 2026

We presented our research at the **Joint 14th International Conference on Soft Computing and Intelligent Systems and 27th International Symposium on Advanced Intelligent Systems (SCIS&ISIS 2026)**, held in Hachioji, Tokyo, from November 2 to 5, 2026.

The presentation reported on the paper below (authors: Chihiro Noda and Ryosuke Saga), which received the **Best Application Award**.

---

### Presentation

- **Evolutionary Edge Bundling: Comparing Large-Scale and Many-Objective Evolutionary Algorithms**  
  Chihiro Noda, Ryosuke Saga

---

### Award

- **SCIS&ISIS2026 Best Application Award**  
  https://scisisis.github.io/scis-isis-2026/award.html

The award recognized this study, which treats evolutionary edge bundling as a large-scale multi-objective optimization problem and compares large-scale optimization methods with many-objective optimization methods on networks with up to 56,895 decision variables.

We will continue to share our research through presentations at international conferences.
