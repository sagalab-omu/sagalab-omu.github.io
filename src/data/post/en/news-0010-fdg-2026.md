## Our paper has been accepted at FDG 2026

A paper by **Oura Uemura** and colleagues from our laboratory has been accepted in the **Game Analytics and Visualization** track of **Foundations of Digital Games 2026 (FDG 2026)**, a leading international conference on game research.

FDG 2026 will be held at the Royal Danish Academy in Copenhagen, Denmark, from August 10 to 13, 2026. The presentation will take place at the conference.

### Accepted Paper

- **A Time Series Causal Analysis of Engagement Factors in Live Streaming Using Continuous-Time Structural Equation Modeling**  
  Oura Uemura, Ryousuke Saga  
  A time series causal analysis of engagement factors in live streaming using continuous-time structural equation modeling

---

### Overview

This study analyzes the dynamic processes that shape viewer engagement in live streaming by combining emotional features derived from speech with chat behavior metrics. Grounded in social presence theory and emotional contagion theory, it models the interaction between streamers and viewers using **Continuous-Time Structural Equation Modeling (CTSEM)**.

We collected data from popular Japanese game streams and converted it into one-minute multimodal segments covering speech frequency, overall vocal intensity, speech sentiment, chat frequency, tipping behavior, and chat sentiment. The CTSEM analysis revealed four main findings:

1. Both viewer engagement and the streamer's verbal expression show rapid, self-decaying autoregressive dynamics
2. The streamer's expressive behavior, reflected in speech frequency, vocal intensity, and emotional tone, has a strong positive effect on subsequent viewer engagement
3. Viewers' collective activity also influences the streamer's expressive behavior, forming a mutual feedback loop
4. The relative strength of these paths suggests that the streamer's emotional cues carry comparatively strong influence in this real-time interactive system

These results show that live streaming is a stable emotional and behavioral system even on short time scales, and they offer practical insights for interactive design and for optimizing streamers' communication strategies.
