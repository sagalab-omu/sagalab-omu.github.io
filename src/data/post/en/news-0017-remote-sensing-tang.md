## Our paper has been published in Remote Sensing

A paper by **Project Assistant Professor Jiansong Tang** and colleagues from our laboratory has been published in **Remote Sensing**, an open-access international journal published by MDPI.

---

### Published Paper

- **Process-Informed Satellite-Ground Fusion for Coastal Compound Humid-Heat and Photochemical Oxidant Early Warning**  
  Jiansong Tang, Ryosuke Saga  
  *Remote Sensing*, Vol. 18, Issue 17, 2026 (published August 25, 2026)  
  DOI: [10.3390/rs18172874](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18172874)

---

### Overview

In coastal areas, **humid heat** caused by high temperature and humidity can occur together with **photochemical oxidants**, a type of air pollutant, making early warning essential for preventing health damage.

This study proposes **Process-Informed Satellite-Ground Fusion**, a method that fuses satellite and ground-based observations based on knowledge of the underlying physical and chemical processes. By using satellite data such as MODIS, it aims to improve prediction accuracy for compound humid-heat and photochemical oxidant events in coastal areas and to make alerts more efficient.
