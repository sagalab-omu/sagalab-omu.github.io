## Our paper has been accepted in IEEE Access

A paper by **Cai Yuqing** and colleagues from our laboratory has been accepted for publication in **IEEE Access**, an open-access international journal published by IEEE.

---

### Accepted Paper

- **Sentiment-Segmented Tri-Training for Fake Review Detection**  
  Yuqing Cai, Ryosuke Saga  
  Fake review detection using tri-training combined with segmentation based on sentiment polarity

---

### Overview

Detecting fake reviews in online reviews is an important challenge for protecting consumers and keeping information trustworthy. This study proposes **Sentiment-Segmented Tri-Training**, a new method that adds a segmentation strategy based on review sentiment polarity (positive or negative) to **Tri-Training**, a semi-supervised learning method.

By training separately on reviews with different emotional tendencies, the method makes more effective use of unlabeled data and improves fake review detection accuracy.
