## Our research was presented orally at AROB 31st

Two papers from our laboratory were accepted and presented orally at the **31st International Symposium on Artificial Life and Robotics (AROB 31st)**, held in 2026 at B-Con Plaza in Beppu, Oita Prefecture.

For details on the symposium, see:  
https://isarob.org/symposium/

Kohei Sugimoto and Chihiro Noda each presented the following work.

---

### Presentations

- **Evaluation of the Temporal Similarity of Clustering Results for 3D Fish Trajectories**  
  Sugimoto Kohei, Ryosuke Saga  
  A study evaluating the temporal consistency of clustering results for 3D fish trajectory data

- **GA-based edge bundling with Bézier-curve representations**  
  Chihiro Noda, Ryosuke Saga  
  A study of an edge bundling method using a genetic algorithm and Bézier-curve representations



---

Both studies drew attention for their new findings in data visualization and time series analysis. We will continue to share our research through presentations at international conferences.
